Projects

Entanglement Entropy and Geometry in Quantum Systems2024 – 2025
Final degree project, Physics · graded 9.5/10 · GitHub · Outreach article (UNED Physics Blog, 2025)
  • Investigated how interaction geometry shapes entanglement in hard-core boson systems, testing the entanglement Area Law.
  • Modelled hopping with a Hubbard Hamiltonian on graphs (periodic chains, bridge and tree geometries); computed ground states by numerical diagonalisation and pairwise entanglement entropies from reduced density matrices.
  • Built the Entanglement Adjacency Matrix (EAM) to compare physical connectivity with quantum correlations, identifying Area Law violations and entanglement monogamy effects. All simulations in Python.
  • Later turned the results into an outreach article for a general audience, published on the UNED Physics Department blog.
MLWeather: Temperature Forecasting with ML2026
Personal project · GitHub · Live app
  • Built an end-to-end forecasting pipeline for Seville on hourly data from the Open-Meteo API.
  • Compared Linear Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, SARIMA and LSTM, evaluated with MAE, RMSE and R²; used lag features, cyclical time encodings and a strict train/test split to avoid data leakage.
  • Shipped an interactive Streamlit app with a modular architecture for exploring and comparing predictions. scikit-learn, XGBoost, Keras.
GNSS + IMU Sensor Fusion Simulator2025
Capstone of the GNSS Academy six-month intensive programme (Jan – Jul 2025: DGNSS, RTK, PPP, SBAS, gLAB) · Python
  • Simulated GNSS measurements with SP3 orbit propagation and realistic error models (ionospheric, tropospheric, clock, multipath), with Antenna Phase Offset and Sagnac corrections.
  • Implemented IMU mechanisation equations in the ECEF frame and a least-squares module for GNSS-only positioning; fused GNSS and IMU data with a Kalman filter.
  • Derived position, velocity and attitude from simulated IMU data, with the coordinate transformations needed for motion tracking.
