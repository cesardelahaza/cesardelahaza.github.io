Projects

Entanglement and geometry in quantum systems

Physics final project · 2024–2025

Does the geometry of a system decide which sites end up entangled? I modelled hard-core bosons on graphs (periodic chains, bridge and tree geometries) with a Hubbard Hamiltonian, computed ground states by numerical diagonalisation, and obtained pairwise entanglement entropies from reduced density matrices. Arranging them into an Entanglement Adjacency Matrix let me compare physical connectivity with quantum correlations and identify Area Law violations and entanglement monogamy effects.

  • Python
  • Numerical diagonalisation
  • Reduced density matrices

MLWeather: temperature forecasting for Seville

2026

End-to-end pipeline on hourly data from the Open-Meteo API. Five models compared (Linear Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, SARIMA and an LSTM) with lag features, cyclical time encodings and a strict train/test split to avoid leakage, evaluated with MAE, RMSE and R². Wrapped in a Streamlit app you can play with (the free hosting puts it to sleep when idle, so it may ask you to wake it up first).
